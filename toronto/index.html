01 — The Studio

Ink that was
drawn for you.

Black Lantern is a private, appointment-only studio built around one rule: nothing goes on skin that wasn't drawn for the person wearing it. Consultations are unhurried, stencils get reworked until they're right, and no artist here takes a booking they don't want to do.

Four chairs. Four specialists. A single sterile suite, hospital-grade autoclave, and single-use everything. If your idea is still half-formed, that's the normal starting point — bring the feeling and we'll find the image.

04 — How it goes

Four steps,
no surprises.

01

Consultation

Free, 30 minutes, in person or video. Bring references, scars, a feeling — whatever you have. We talk placement, scale and what will still read in twenty years.

02

The drawing

Your artist draws the piece from scratch. You see it before the machine ever comes out, and revisions are part of the price, not an extra.

03

The session

Stencil placed and re-placed until you're happy with it. Sessions run three to six hours with real breaks. Big work is booked as a series.

04

Healing

You leave with a written aftercare sheet and a direct line to your artist. A free touch-up check sits on the calendar at six weeks.
