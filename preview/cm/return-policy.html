Refund & Return Policy

30-day returns, including damaged goods.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

30 days from delivery

Damaged goods are accepted for return. Customers pay the return shipping cost, including for damaged goods, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Request a return within 30 days

Contact C&M within 30 calendar days after delivery to request a product return. Include your order or invoice number, the item you wish to return, and the reason for the return.

Damaged goods

If your item arrives damaged, contact us within the 30-day return period. Damaged goods are eligible for return. Describe the damage and, if available, include photos of the item and packaging so we can arrange the return.

Return instructions and shipping

Contact us before shipping your item so we can provide the correct return address and instructions. Customers are responsible for return shipping charges, including when returning damaged goods, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Package the item securely and keep your shipment tracking information. Include the original packaging and supplied accessories if available.

Refunds

After we receive and review the returned item, we will contact you about the refund. Refunds are issued to the original payment method where available. The time for a refund to appear depends on the payment provider and financial institution.

Installation and project work

This product-return policy does not set cancellation or refund terms for installation, inspections, or project work. Refer to the written agreement for that work or contact C&M about your project.
