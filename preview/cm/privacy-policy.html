Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with C&M.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Information you provide

When you contact C&M, request a quote, or ask about a product or service, you may provide your name, organization, email address, phone number, facility or delivery address, and details about your request. Messages you enter into the website assistant are also information you provide.

How information is used

We use information associated with your inquiry or transaction to respond, prepare quotes, arrange products or services, provide customer support, and handle returns. Information may also be used to maintain business records, protect against misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Website assistant

Marcus is an automated assistant. Messages submitted to Marcus are sent to service providers that operate the assistant and generate its replies. Avoid entering payment card numbers, passwords, or sensitive personal information into chat. Contact C&M directly for a confirmed quote, order decision, or return arrangement.

Technical information and browser storage

Website hosting and other online service providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, requested pages, and request times as part of delivering the website. Some pages use browser storage to remember interface choices, such as whether a prompt has already been shown. Your browser settings allow you to clear or restrict stored data; doing so may affect those features.

Pages may load fonts, images, or other resources from third-party providers. Those providers receive the connection information needed to deliver their resources.

Service providers and disclosures

Information needed to handle your request or transaction may be shared with providers involved in website hosting, email, automated assistance, order fulfillment, delivery, and payment processing when applicable. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud, security issues, or legal claims. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices.

Payment information

Use only the payment method or payment instructions confirmed for your order. Do not send full payment card details through ordinary email, quote forms, or the website assistant.

Your questions and choices

Contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction or deletion, or tell us your communication preferences. We may need to verify your request, and some records may need to be retained for transaction, accounting, or legal purposes. Any rights available under applicable law remain in effect.
